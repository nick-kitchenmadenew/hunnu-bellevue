## What "Bathroom Remodeling" Means at Hunnu Construction

A bathroom remodel can mean one thing changing or all of them. Hunnu Construction has run both kinds since 2018 — a single vanity swap into a bathroom that's staying otherwise the same, and full renovations that take a bathroom down to the subfloor and rebuild it around a new layout. What doesn't change is who does the work: one team handles the design consultation, the vanity, the tile, the flooring and any shower or tub work, rather than the homeowner coordinating a different specialist for each.

That matters most in the handoffs, the same way it does in a kitchen. A floor sealed before the shower pan is set, a vanity installed before the wall tile height is confirmed, a schedule where the tile setter and the plumber both show up the same day because nobody was tracking the other — these are the failure points of a bathroom run by committee. Sequencing the trades in the right order, with one team accountable for how they fit together, is most of what "project management" means on a bathroom job the same as it does in a kitchen.

## A Second Room, the Same Crew

Hunnu Construction is based in Bellevue and has worked across the Eastside and into Seattle since 2018 — the same service area for bathroom work as for kitchens. A bathroom's failure points differ from a kitchen's in specifics — moisture and waterproofing matter more here than almost anywhere else in the house — but the underlying approach is the same: an in-home consultation to measure the actual space, not a generic layout, followed by one crew carrying the project through to the final walkthrough.

Households already familiar with Hunnu Construction's kitchen work sometimes ask whether the same crew handles bathrooms too. It does — the two are run as separate projects with separate scopes, but the same design-consultation-first approach, the same single point of contact, and the same one-quote-covers-everything model apply to both.
