## Kitchen Remodeling in Bothell

Bothell is one of the few cities in the service area split across two counties, King and Snohomish, and it's been part of Hunnu Construction's territory since 2018 alongside the rest of the Eastside. The work doesn't change based on which side of that county line a particular kitchen sits — the same design consultation, the same crew, and the same sequence of cabinets, countertops, flooring and backsplash apply regardless.

A kitchen that's aged along with an established Bothell neighborhood tends to need the same honest assessment any older kitchen does: whether the layout still works, whether the cabinets are structurally sound or just outdated, and whether one surface has failed while the rest of the kitchen is fine. That assessment is what a design consultation is for, and it's the same starting point whether the project turns out to be a full renovation or a single trade replaced on its own.

## Planning a Bothell Kitchen Project

Every project in Bothell starts with an in-home visit — measuring the actual space, discussing what isn't working about the current kitchen, and scoping the project honestly rather than assuming it needs to be bigger than it is. From there, cabinets, countertops, flooring and backsplash are quoted as one project if that's what the kitchen needs, or as a single trade if that's all that's actually required.

The sequence a Bothell kitchen follows is the same one every kitchen in the service area follows: cabinets set first, since everything else in the room gets measured against them; countertops templated once those cabinets are confirmed in place; flooring and backsplash finishing the room in the order that protects the surfaces already installed. One crew carries that sequence from the first measurement to the final walkthrough, rather than handing pieces of it off between separate contractors scheduling around their own calendars.

## Two Counties, One Process

Sitting across a county line doesn't come up much in day-to-day life, but it can matter to a remodel if part of the project needs a permit — King County and Snohomish County run separate permitting offices, and which one applies depends on exactly where the address falls. That's a detail worth raising early rather than assuming, since it can affect timeline depending on how the specific project is scoped.

Outside of that one administrative wrinkle, a Bothell project runs no differently on either side of the line. The same crew, the same design consultation, and the same sequence of trades apply whether the kitchen sits in the King County half of the city or the Snohomish County half — the county line is a fact worth knowing, not a factor that changes how the work actually gets done.

## Families, Established Streets and What Changes Over Time

Bothell has a lot of settled, family-oriented neighborhoods where a house has often been owned by one family for a long stretch of time, long enough that the kitchen was last updated for a different set of needs than the household actually has now. A layout built for a smaller family, or for a kitchen used differently than it is today, is a common starting point for a Bothell design consultation — not because anything is broken, but because how the room gets used has changed more than the room has.

That's a different starting question than "what's failed," and it's one the design consultation is built to handle just as well. Sometimes the answer is a full renovation that reworks the layout; sometimes it's simply new cabinets and a counter that make the existing footprint work better for how the kitchen is actually used now. Either way, the same crew carries the project from that first conversation through to the final walkthrough, whichever answer the honest assessment lands on.

A kitchen that's genuinely still working well structurally, just aesthetically behind, is also a common Bothell starting point — cabinets that are solid but dated, a countertop that's intact but no longer matches anything else in the house. That's often the fastest and least disruptive kind of project, since it doesn't touch the layout or anything behind the walls at all.
