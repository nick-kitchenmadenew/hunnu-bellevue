## What "Kitchen Remodeling" Means at Hunnu Construction

A kitchen remodel can mean one thing changing or all of them. Hunnu Construction has run both kinds since 2018 — a single new countertop set into cabinets that are staying, and full renovations that take a kitchen down to the studs and rebuild it around a new layout. What doesn't change is who does the work: one team handles the design consultation, the cabinets, the countertop, the floor and the backsplash, rather than the homeowner coordinating a different specialist for each.

That matters most in the handoffs. A countertop templated to cabinets that later shift, a floor cut around a backsplash installed before it, a schedule where three trades all show up the same week because nobody was tracking the other two — these are the failure points of a remodel run by committee. Sequencing the trades in the right order, and having one team accountable for how they fit together, is most of what "project management" means on a kitchen job.

## Serving Bellevue and the Eastside Since 2018

Hunnu Construction is based in Bellevue and has worked from there since 2018, serving the Eastside — Renton, Bothell, Redmond, Issaquah, Kirkland, Sammamish, Shoreline and Woodinville — along with the Seattle neighborhoods of Ballard and Magnolia. Kitchens differ by neighborhood in ways that matter to a remodel: a mid-century Bellevue rambler and a newer Sammamish build don't have the same wall layouts or cabinet depths behind them, and a crew that has worked across the area brings that with them to the first walkthrough.

Every project still starts the same way regardless of city — an in-home consultation to measure the actual space, not a generic layout. What differs is logistics: parking, building access, and whether the block allows a dumpster at the curb during demolition, all of which get worked out before the job starts rather than discovered on day one.
