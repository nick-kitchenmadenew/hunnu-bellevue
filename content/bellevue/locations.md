## One Crew Across the Whole Service Area

Every city Hunnu Construction serves gets the same starting point: an in-home design consultation to measure the actual kitchen and talk through what it needs, followed by one crew carrying the project through cabinets, countertops, flooring and backsplash — on its own or together as a full renovation. What changes from city to city isn't the service, it's the kitchens themselves — an older Seattle home raises different layout questions than a newer Sammamish build, and a Woodinville property on well water has different plumbing considerations than a Kirkland waterfront lot.

That's why each city below has its own page rather than one generic service-area description repeated eleven times. The sequence a project follows — cabinets confirmed first, countertops templated to them, flooring and backsplash finishing the room — stays the same everywhere. What's worth knowing about the specific city it's happening in doesn't.

## Bellevue and the Eastside

Hunnu Construction is based in Bellevue and has worked from there since 2018. The Eastside cities in the service area — Renton, Bothell, Redmond, Issaquah, Kirkland, Sammamish, Shoreline and Woodinville — each have their own mix of older, established neighborhoods and newer construction, and that mix is exactly what a design consultation is built to assess honestly rather than assume from the outside. A newer Sammamish or Issaquah kitchen more often needs an upgrade to builder-grade finishes; an older Bothell or Renton kitchen more often raises the layout question.

Distance from Bellevue varies across that list too, from a short drive to the farther edge of the service area near Shoreline. None of that changes the service itself — the same design consultation, the same one crew, and the same single quote apply regardless of which Eastside city the kitchen is in. What it does change is scheduling and material logistics, which get planned for up front rather than discovered as a delay partway through a project.

## Seattle Neighborhoods

Seattle is close enough to Hunnu Construction's Bellevue base to be part of the working service area, with Ballard and Magnolia as the two neighborhoods worked in most consistently, alongside the rest of the city on a case-by-case basis. Seattle's older housing stock raises the layout question more often than newer Eastside construction does — a kitchen built decades ago was designed around how kitchens were used then, and a remodel there is frequently as much about whether the layout itself should change as it is about which finishes go in.

Ballard and Magnolia each have their own character within that broader pattern. Ballard's craftsman-era homes tend toward smaller, more compact kitchens where storage and layout efficiency matter more than they would in a larger, newer build. Magnolia's quieter residential streets mean a kitchen remodel there is more often a long-planned decision than an urgent one, which changes the pacing of the design consultation more than the scope of the work itself.
