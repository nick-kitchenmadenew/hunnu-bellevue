## Kitchen Remodeling for Renton Homeowners

Renton sits at the south end of Lake Washington, close enough to Bellevue that a design consultation there is no more of a logistical question than one across town. Hunnu Construction has served Renton as part of its Eastside service area since 2018, working the same way there as everywhere else — an in-home visit to measure the actual kitchen, followed by one crew carrying the project through cabinets, countertops, flooring and backsplash, whether that's one trade on its own or all four together as a full renovation.

What doesn't change by city is the sequence a kitchen remodel actually runs on: cabinets confirmed first, since counters get templated to them and backsplash tile stops where they end; flooring and backsplash finishing the room once the surfaces above them are set. A Renton kitchen isn't treated any differently in that regard than a kitchen in Bellevue itself — the same crew, the same sequence, the same one point of contact from the first measurement to the final walkthrough.

## What to Expect for a Renton Kitchen Project

The starting point for any Renton project is the same design consultation every kitchen gets — an in-home visit to talk through what's actually not working in the current kitchen, whether that's the layout, the cabinets, the countertop, or all of the above, and to measure the space against what's actually possible rather than a generic plan. From there, the project is scoped and quoted as one job, not assembled from separate estimates for separate trades.

Renton's distance from Bellevue is short enough that it doesn't add meaningfully to a project's timeline — material still needs to be ordered, cabinets still need to be templated and fabricated where relevant, and the same sequence of demolition, rough-in, installation and walkthrough applies regardless of which Eastside city the kitchen is in. A homeowner in Renton gets the same design consultation, the same one crew, and the same single quote covering the whole project as a homeowner anywhere else in the service area.

## From First Call to Final Walkthrough

A Renton project moves through the same stages every kitchen remodel does, regardless of size. The design consultation comes first, followed by a single quote once the scope is actually set. If the project involves demolition, that's where work physically starts, followed by any rough-in changes the layout needs, then the visible trades — cabinets, counters, flooring, backsplash — in the sequence each depends on.

The final walkthrough is the last stage, and it's treated as a real step rather than a formality — going through the finished kitchen together, checking that everything discussed at the design consultation actually made it into the finished room, and working through any punch-list items before the job is called done. A homeowner in Renton gets that same closing step as anywhere else in the service area, not a project that's considered finished the moment the crew packs up its tools and drives away for the last time.

## A Kitchen That Fits How the House Is Actually Used

Renton covers a genuinely wide range of housing — established neighborhoods with decades-old kitchens, newer construction near the lake, and everything between. What a remodel actually needs to accomplish differs across that range more than the city name suggests: a kitchen in an older home is more likely to need the layout itself reconsidered, while a newer one more often needs an upgrade to cabinets or a countertop that were chosen to a builder's budget rather than a homeowner's taste.

That's exactly why the design consultation happens in the home rather than over the phone. A quote based on square footage or a generic "kitchen remodel" category would treat a cosmetic cabinet swap the same as a full layout reconfiguration, and the two are different projects with different timelines and different costs. Measuring the actual kitchen first is what keeps the scope — and the price — honest to what the house in front of it actually needs.
