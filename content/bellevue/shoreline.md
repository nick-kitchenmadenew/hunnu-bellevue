## Kitchen Remodeling in Shoreline

Shoreline sits just north of Seattle, on the farther edge of Hunnu Construction's service area from its Bellevue base — and it's been served the same way as every closer city since 2018. The distance doesn't change the process: an in-home design consultation to measure the actual kitchen, one crew across every trade the project needs, and one quote covering the whole job rather than a bill from each contractor involved.

What distance does change is worth being upfront about: a Shoreline project is scheduled with that travel factored in from the start, rather than treated as identical logistics to a Bellevue kitchen a few minutes away. That's a scheduling detail, not a service difference — the same design consultation, the same crew, and the same sequence of trades apply regardless of which end of the service area the kitchen is in.

## Planning a Shoreline Kitchen Project

A Shoreline project starts the same way any project does: measuring the kitchen, discussing what isn't working, and scoping the work honestly before anything is quoted. Whether that turns out to be a full renovation or a single trade — cabinets, countertop, flooring or backsplash on its own — depends on the kitchen itself, not on the distance from Bellevue.

Once the scope is set, the sequence is the same one every kitchen in the service area follows: cabinets confirmed first, countertops templated once they're set, flooring and backsplash finishing the job last. One crew carries that sequence the whole way, which matters more for a project farther from base — fewer separate contractors' schedules to coordinate across a longer drive, not more.

## Why Distance Argues for One Crew, Not Against It

Coordinating four separate contractors gets harder, not easier, the farther a project sits from where those contractors are each based — a delay that's a minor inconvenience for a nearby job becomes a bigger scheduling problem when a specialist has to make the drive out again to pick up where they left off. A single crew that stays on site through the whole project sidesteps that entirely, since there's no second trip to coordinate once demolition and rough-in are underway.

That's part of the practical case for handling every trade with one team on a project farther from Bellevue: fewer separate arrivals to schedule, fewer gaps in the work while a different specialist gets free, and one crew accountable for the whole timeline rather than four each accountable for their own piece of it.

## Ordering Material Early for a Longer Commute

Material lead time matters more for a Shoreline project than for one closer to Bellevue, not because the material itself takes longer to arrive, but because a delivery mistake or a wrong measurement caught early is a short trip to fix nearby and a longer one for a project on the far edge of the service area. That's exactly why templates, measurements and orders get confirmed carefully before anything is placed, rather than adjusted on the fly once material has already arrived on site.

None of that changes the timeline in a way a homeowner would notice — it's a planning detail on Hunnu Construction's side, not something that adds days to a Shoreline project versus one closer to home. The design consultation accounts for it up front, the same way it accounts for everything else that affects a specific project's schedule.

It's also why a Shoreline project benefits from a firm scope decided early rather than one that keeps shifting as work goes — a mid-project change to material or layout is a bigger schedule hit the farther that change has to travel to be sourced and delivered, which is exactly the kind of decision worth locking in during the design consultation rather than after demolition has already started on site.

None of this is a reason to hesitate about booking a project on the far side of the service area — it's simply how that distance gets planned around rather than ignored, and it's the same reason a single accountable crew matters more, not less, the farther a kitchen sits from Bellevue. Distance is a scheduling detail Hunnu Construction plans for, not a reason to say no to a Shoreline project.
