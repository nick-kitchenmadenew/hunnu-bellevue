# Meeting Notes: GMB Categories & Client Management

## Main Content

The call focused on high-level strategy for Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization, managing client expectations, and effective lead generation for SEO agencies.

### Google Business Profile (GBP) Strategy
*   **Secondary Categories:** Simply adding secondary categories does not improve rankings. Each secondary category requires a dedicated landing page with unique, relevant content and a proper internal linking structure to rank effectively.
*   **Service Area Businesses (SABs):** Ranking an SAB (without a visible address) is significantly harder (30-50%) than a business with a physical location. Agencies should charge higher fees for these clients to account for the additional effort required.
*   **Changes & Re-verification:** Making major updates to a GBP (name, address, category) can trigger suspension or re-verification. Use management tools like Lead Snap or have a third-party "suggest" changes to reduce risk.

### Client Management & Boundaries
*   **Managing Demanding Clients:** High-paying clients often demand excessive time. Caleb advises setting clear boundaries regarding communication time in the initial contract. Use data visualization tools (e.g., heat maps) to educate clients on their actual ranking radius rather than engaging in endless phone calls.
*   **Firing Clients:** It is sometimes necessary to "fire" clients who refuse to implement necessary changes (like adding content to their website) or who consume too much time, as they are not a good fit for SEO work.

### Website Structure & Content
*   **The "Burn Down" Strategy:** For very old, thin, or non-performing websites, it is often more efficient to "burn the site down" and relaunch it rather than trying to salvage an amateur, outdated WordPress site.
*   **Content Strategy:** Websites must have content to rank for non-branded terms. 
    *   **Blogs vs. Supporting Pages:** The term "blog" is misleading. Agencies should not waste time on generic listicles. Instead, create articles that build topical and geographic relevance (e.g., "new service in Phoenix") to link directly to main service pages.
    *   **Hierarchy:** The platform (WordPress, Wix, HighLevel) matters less than the linking structure. Focus on supporting content that links to main service pages for Google’s crawlers, not just for human users.

### Lead Generation & Outreach
*   **Webinars:** Weekly webinars are an effective lead generation tool for agencies. They allow you to share expertise and build trust, which is a necessary step before pitching services.
*   **Local Authority:** Build geographic relevance by joining local Chambers of Commerce and sponsoring local organizations (e.g., youth sports leagues). 
*   **AI Tools:** Participants noted that different LLMs yield different results; for instance, Gemini was reported as particularly effective for generating local backlink strategies compared to other models.
