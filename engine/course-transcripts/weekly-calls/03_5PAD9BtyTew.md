*   **The "Lazy Ranking Method":** This methodology prioritizes high-impact, low-effort tasks:

    *   Ensure title tags match the primary service.
    *   Use AI to automate content creation (GBP posts, review responses) and photo management.
    *   The goal is to get "good enough" results to help local businesses rank within their service area without over-engineering every page.
*   **Technical SEO and Schema:** Caleb emphasizes that while schema markup and page load speed are ranking factors, they are secondary. Schema is "easy to implement" and good for AI context (ChatGPT/Claude), but doesn't require obsessive optimization.

For agencies, scaling is achieved through "snapshots" in platforms like HighLevel (GHL), allowing developers to reuse website structures across clients in the same niche.

The consensus is that Anthropic’s Claude consistently outperforms ChatGPT in content quality, despite varying model updates. The advice is to prioritize speed and efficiency—if a tool like Page Optimizer Pro saves time, use it to produce more content rather than seeking perfection.
