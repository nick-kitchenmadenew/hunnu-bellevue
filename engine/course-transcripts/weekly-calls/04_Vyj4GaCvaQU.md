## Main Content

This session was a weekly community call focused on practical local SEO strategies, primarily revolving around the "Core 30" content framework, Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization, and the use of "Local Rank Maps" (heat maps) to guide strategy.

**Core SEO & Content Strategy**
*   **The "Core 30":** This strategy focuses on building topical relevance through service and category pages. It is recommended to build this for every location a business serves until the domain achieves sufficient topical authority.
*   **Content Creation:** With AI, content generation costs have dropped significantly (under half a penny per word). The host advises against repurposing content across locations; instead, generate unique content for each city/location to avoid indexing issues.
*   **Site Structure:** Keep navigation bars concise (under 20–30 links). URL structure (folders vs. flat) is considered less important than the internal linking structure. Avoid deleting existing content unless the site is completely failing to rank, as Google will eventually re-crawl and index changes.
*   **August 2025 Update:** A major talking point was a recent Google update that penalizes "city pages" (pages targeting a specific city without a corresponding physical Google Business Profile in that city). 

**Local Ranking & Heat Maps**
*   **Rank Maps:** The host identifies local rank maps as the #1 SEO tool. They suggest running a 13x13 grid at 0.5–1 mile intervals.
*   **Keyword Tracking:** Don't waste credits tracking 50+ keyword variations. Track the primary category + 2-3 core services. If a site ranks for "lawyer," it will likely rank for "attorney" as well.
*   **Multi-City Ranking:** Ranking a single GBP in multiple neighboring cities is extremely difficult. Google heavily weighs the city of the searcher against the city of the GBP. If competitors are ranking in multiple cities, it may be possible, but otherwise, separate GBPs are necessary.

**Backlinks & Authority**
*   **Local Links:** The strategy for backlinks remains focused on local, low-cost opportunities ($100–$200 range). Joining local Chambers of Commerce (even those 70 miles away) and sponsoring local youth sports leagues are high-value, cost-effective activities. Avoid expensive, high-ticket backlinks.

**Tools & Workflow**
*   **Tech Stack:** The agency uses a specific, streamlined stack: LeadSnap (for rank maps), HighLevel (website building), ChatGPT/Claude (content), Screaming Frog, and Page Optimizer Pro (POP).
*   **Reporting:** The host has abandoned traditional, complex SEO reporting in favor of simple, visual proof: comparing the local rank map from the day of hire versus the current day.
