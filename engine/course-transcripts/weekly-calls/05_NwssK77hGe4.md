## Main Content

The September 30, 2025, call focused on optimizing content production workflows, team management for scaling, and specific tactics for Google Business Profile (GBP) and local SEO.

### Content Production System
*   **Workflow Efficiency:** The host shared updates to the internal copywriting process. By moving from lengthy, 40-page documentation to concise, video-based training, production time per article was cut from 45–60 minutes down to 25–30 minutes.
*   **Team Roles & Structure:**
    *   **SEO Expert (Host):** Identifies target keywords and creates overall strategy.
    *   **Copywriter Manager:** Assigns tasks based on capacity, reviews content quality, manages revisions, and ensures all SEO elements (internal/external links) are present.
    *   **Copywriters:** Write the content (aiming for 1,000–1,500 words). Paid $30/article for the first 10 (onboarding/training) and $10/article thereafter.
    *   **Schema & Image Specialist:** Adds images (5–10 per page) and schema markup (paid $5 per article).
    *   **Developer:** Handles final implementation on client websites (paid hourly).
*   **Cost Management:** Total production cost per article is roughly $90–$100, which covers copywriting, schema/images, and development.

### SEO & GBP Tactics
*   **Client Communication:** New clients require approval on content, while long-term clients allow direct posting. The host utilizes an automated macro in Outlook to remind him to follow up on pending client approvals after 72 hours.
*   **GBP Verification:** Adding or changing an address or business name frequently triggers a reverification requirement. The host advises creating "sticky" verification videos: under 2 minutes, no faces, no motion blur, and filmed during off-peak times.
*   **Ranking & Strategy:**
    *   **Core 30:** Continues to be the foundational strategy for new businesses.
    *   **Geographic Focus:** Rankings are determined by local competition, not just population. Analyzing competitor rank maps is critical before building out geographic pages.
    *   **Site Stability:** If a GBP is ranking well, the host strongly advises against making major changes (like URL swaps), as it risks losing position.
    *   **FAQ/Schema:** FAQ blocks should be plain text paragraphs under headers (no JS-enabled dropdowns) to maximize crawlability.

### Client Acquisition
*   **Networking:** The host suggests attending local Chamber of Commerce events and engaging in Facebook groups/Reddit. The goal is to provide value by running rank maps, performing a 10-minute site audit, and offering to fix simple technical issues (like Title/H1 tags) for free to build trust.
