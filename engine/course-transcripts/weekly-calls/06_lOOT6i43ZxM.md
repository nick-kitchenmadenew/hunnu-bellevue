## Main Content

### Internal Linking & Website Structure
*   **Editorial vs. Navigation:** The host emphasized that navigation links have minimal SEO impact. Efforts should be focused on editorial links within the content body.
*   **Linking Ratios:** Internal anchor text should follow a ratio of roughly 2/3 branded/naked URLs and 1/3 keyword-rich text.
*   **Hierarchy:** The recommended structure is: Homepage → Category → Services → Supporting Content (FAQs). Supporting content should be linked from relevant FAQs at the bottom of service pages.

### Content Creation Workflow (AI-Integrated)
*   **Efficiency:** The goal is to reduce article creation time from 45+ minutes to 15–20 minutes using Claude Projects.
*   **The Process:** 
    1. Generate an outline using specific templates for the page type (category, service, neighborhood, etc.).
    2. Write the content using a client-specific project prompt containing business details and constraints.
    3. **Crucial Step:** Use a dedicated "Fact-Check" prompt to force the AI to verify its own content. 
    4. Insert links and finalize.

### GBP Optimization & Local Strategy
*   **Primary Categories:** Choosing the primary category is the single most important decision. For example, in warmer climates, HVAC businesses should prioritize "Air Conditioning Repair" over "HVAC Contractor" based on search intent.
*   **Fields:** Every field in the GBP must be filled out. Incomplete profiles are a primary reason for low rankings.
*   **Rank Maps:** Do not run rank maps for every service. Focus on the primary category and the 2–3 most critical services. 
*   **Location Pages:** Only build location-specific pages when rank maps indicate a specific need (e.g., areas showing 4s, 5s, and 6s on the heat map).

### External Linking Strategy
*   **Two Types of Links:**
    1. **"This isn't slop" links:** Used to ensure content indexing and inform Google that the site is legitimate, not AI-generated spam.
    2. **Trust links:** High-authority links from Chamber of Commerce, sponsorships, and community events that provide real community standing.

### Franchise Challenges
*   When a client is restricted to a national franchise website, the strategy shifts to building an independent satellite site structure (following the "Core 30") to serve as an anchor for relevance. While this takes more work than having full domain access, it allows for better control over topical authority.
