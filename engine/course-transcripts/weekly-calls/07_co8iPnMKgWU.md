## Main Content

The community call focused on the progress of an active "challenge" where the host is testing a Facebook ad funnel for local SEO services. The discussion highlighted several core operational and strategic areas:

### Facebook Ad Funnel & Strategy
*   **Performance:** Caleb reported an average cost-per-lead (CPL) of $8–$13. While the offer is functional, conversion rates from traffic to signups remain a primary focus for optimization.
*   **Retargeting:** Participants identified that retargeting campaigns are essential, as most initial Facebook ad campaigns lose money until a retargeting layer is added.
*   **Trust Building:** There was a debate on whether to use real client names for social proof. Caleb advised against this, citing past experiences where sharing real domains on public videos triggered DDoS attacks or harassment against his clients. He advocates for using guarantees and logical service explanations instead.

### Local SEO Methodology
*   **"Lazy" vs. "Core 30":** The primary SEO offer being tested uses a simplified setup (Google Business Profile (GBP) management, correct category/service selection, landing page edits) rather than the comprehensive "Core 30" methodology. Caleb argues that for most cities under 300,000 in population, this simplified approach is sufficient to jump from 5% top-3 ranking to 40–50%.
*   **Geographic vs. Topical Relevance:** The group discussed how to differentiate content. Topical relevance focuses on "service + city" keywords, whereas geographical relevance involves "service + local landmark + city" and includes driving directions from the landmark to the business.
*   **Backlink Strategy:** Caleb explicitly rejects traditional backlink analysis, claiming it is a waste of time. Instead, he focuses on securing high-trust, hyper-local links (e.g., sponsoring local TEDx events).

### Troubleshooting & Sales
*   **"Ranking but No Calls":** A common issue discussed was when a site ranks highly but generates no calls. Caleb recommends:
    1.  Verifying the tracking phone number is actually set up and forwarding correctly (GBP data is often unreliable).
    2.  Ensuring the business owner answers the phone.
    3.  Implementing AI voice agents (via Go High Level) to handle missed calls.
*   **Real Estate/Professional Services:** For high-ticket services (real estate/financial planning), SEO should shift away from "near me" keywords toward neighborhood-specific content and building mailing lists, as clients in these niches rarely find providers through generic search.
