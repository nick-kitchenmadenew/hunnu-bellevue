## Main Content

The call was a deep dive into local SEO, specifically focusing on Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization, technical SEO for local businesses, and efficient AI-driven content workflows.

**Google Business Profile & Local Strategy**
*   **Primary Categories:** A major emphasis was placed on choosing the *correct* primary category based on actual consumer search behavior, not just industry descriptors (e.g., using "Air Conditioning Repair" instead of "HVAC Contractor" for better lead conversion).
*   **Local Ranking Factors:** The discussion highlighted the three pillars of local ranking: Proximity, Relevance (topical and geographical), and Trust. The goal is to expand the "green zone" on rank maps.
*   **Account Safety:** The host stressed locking down GBP profiles to prevent unauthorized changes by team members, which can inadvertently delete secondary categories and cause ranking drops.

**Linking Strategies**
*   **Internal Linking:** A structured approach is recommended: Homepage → Category Pages → Service Pages (bidirectional).
*   **External Authority:** Include 2-3 links to high-authority sites (like Harvard or Forbes) per 500-700 words. This improves indexing, builds trust, and helps lower AI-detection scores.
*   **Off-site Authority:** For links *back* to the business, focus on local trust signals like Chamber of Commerce sponsorships or local event sponsorships.

**Content Creation & AI Workflows**
*   **Efficient Production:** The host detailed a process where AI (Claude) generates content, which is then human-edited for quality. A ready-to-post article can be produced in about 20 minutes.
*   **Customization:** Use custom "Projects" in Claude for each client to maintain consistent tone and strictly enforce "do not mention" rules (e.g., specific prohibited services).
*   **Workflow:** An efficient team setup involves an AI editor (who handles the content/links/humanizing), an overseas assistant (for image generation, compression, and schema), and a developer for final posting.

**Addressing Competitor Analysis**
*   The host advised against over-analyzing competitors, noting that "goal completion" (customer phone calls/satisfaction) often overrides technical metrics. Inconsistent local rankings are frequently due to historical performance rather than just SEO tactics.
