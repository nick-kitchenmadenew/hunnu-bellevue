## Main Content

### Local SEO Strategy & Core 30 Framework
*   **The "No Keyword Research" Rule:** For local clients, keyword research is deemed unnecessary. The primary focus is ranking the Google Business Profile (GBP) for the business’s primary category and city.
*   **Content Strategy:** The Core 30 framework involves building topical relevance through a structured website. Start with a foundational audit and landing page optimization, then scale content production (starting with one article per month and increasing as needed).
*   **PBN Usage:** Despite the trend toward "white hat" only, PBN links remain a core part of the agency's strategy. Every piece of published content receives one PBN link to serve as a "validation signal" to Google.
*   **Website Structure:** Website structure should mirror the GBP category/service structure. If a business needs to rank for multiple distinct categories (e.g., Plumbing and HVAC), use primary/secondary category structures on the GBP rather than splitting them into separate domains.

### GBP Management & Technical Risks
*   **Re-verification Dangers:** Any change to a GBP (name, address, category) carries a high risk of triggering re-verification. Caleb advises having all documentation ready and suggests having a "Local Guide" (a separate Google account with high review activity) suggest category changes to minimize suspension risk.
*   **Service Area Businesses (SABs):** These are significantly harder to rank than businesses with visible, verified physical addresses.
*   **Website Redesigns:** Avoid complete website redesigns, as they often negatively impact ranking. Stick to adding content and optimizing existing pages.

### Tool Stack
*   **Claude:** The preferred tool for content generation (Sonnet) and data analysis (Opus).
*   **Lead Snap:** Recommended for citations ($20/month).
*   **Page Optimizer Pro:** Essential for optimizing landing pages in competitive markets.
*   **Ahrefs:** Explicitly discouraged for local SEO. Caleb noted he has not subscribed in months, stating it is a "waste of money" for local-only agencies.

### Agency Operations
*   **Workflow:** New processes are performed personally by the agency owner first, then recorded via Loom, and finally delegated to employees/contractors.
*   **Scaling:** Caleb announced an upcoming "10K Challenge"—a 3-month project to scale an SEO agency to $10k/month without sales calls.
