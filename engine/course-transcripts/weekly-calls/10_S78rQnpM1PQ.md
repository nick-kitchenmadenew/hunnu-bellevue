# Meeting Notes: AI SEO & LLM Landing Pages (August 7, 2025)

## Main Content

This weekly call focused on advanced strategies for local SEO, specifically bridging the gap between traditional Google-focused SEO and the emerging needs of AI-powered search engines (LLMs like ChatGPT).

**Core Themes:**
*   **AI/LLM Search Optimization:** The host emphasized that ranking for LLMs is distinct from ranking for Google. While Google favors traditional SEO metrics, LLMs place heavy weight on structured data (Schema), specific brand mentions, and social proof.
*   **LLM Landing Pages:** The discussion introduced the concept of creating landing pages specifically optimized for AI consumption rather than just human conversion. Key elements include detailed FAQ sections, embedded review widgets (which LLMs parse for specific service mentions), and clear business/owner biographies. The goal is to provide a "structured" source of truth for the AI to "read" and ingest.
*   **Website Sensitivity:** A recurring warning throughout the call was the danger of altering established websites. Even minor design changes, hosting moves, or restructuring can lead to significant, long-term ranking drops (3+ months for recovery). The advice is: *if you are ranking well, do not change existing elements; only add content.*
*   **Local SEO Tactics:** 
    *   **GBP Posts:** These are considered low-impact for human traffic but are useful for signaling activity to Google. They do not require elaborate strategy.
    *   **Citations:** Tools like *Lead Snap* are recommended for automated citation building, though the process requires patience (1–2 weeks).
    *   **Indexing:** Reasonable indexing time for a healthy site is about one week. If issues persist, the host suggests checking internal linking/sitemaps and utilizing tools like *Index Meow*.
    *   **Reddit Strategy:** Participation in local subreddits (AMAs, helpful advice) is becoming a high-value signal for both Google and LLMs, as they value community-driven content.
