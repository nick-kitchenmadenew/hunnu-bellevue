# Applied principles — what we have learned by doing the work

What **we** have concluded from applying the Core 30, as distinct from what
the course teaches. Empty of entries by design at creation: nothing belongs
here until it has actually earned its place by the test below.

## The three tiers, and why this file is separate

| Tier | Source of truth | Lives in |
|---|---|---|
| What the course teaches | Caleb's lessons (verbatim) and calls | `CORE30-METHODOLOGY.md`, `CORE30-FAQ.md` |
| **What we have validated ourselves** | **Our own work across clients** | **This file** |
| What is true for one client only | That engagement | That site's `OPERATIONS.md` / `DISCREPANCIES.md` |

Keeping these apart is the whole point. Course canon must stay a faithful
record — the moment our opinion is edited into it, we can no longer tell
what Caleb actually said from what we decided. And a one-client quirk
promoted to a general principle is worse than useless: it gets applied to
the next client, where it is wrong.

## The promotion test

Before anything is written here, it must pass this:

**Has it happened more than once, independently?** A second client, a second
location, a second market. One occurrence is an anecdote. Two independent
occurrences are a pattern.

A finding may also qualify without a second occurrence if it is a
**demonstrated correction** to something the canon states — not a
preference, but a case where following the course material as written
produced a wrong result and we can show why.

Everything else fails the test. A one-off constraint — this client's
franchise agreement, that city's licensing rules, a particular owner's
preference — is **not** a candidate. It stays with that engagement, where it
is true, and nothing about it needs to change.

## Archive — rejected and superseded

Rejected candidates and revised principles get a one-line note here rather
than deletion. Cheap to keep, and it stops the same idea being re-proposed
and re-argued a year later by someone who was not in the original
conversation — the job `DISCREPANCIES.md` already does well on the site
side.
